# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how this task management application collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our service.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide directly

- **Account information**: name, email address, and password when you sign up
- **Profile information**: display name
- **Task data**: titles, descriptions, due dates, and status of tasks you create

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Session data**: authentication tokens and session identifiers to keep you logged in
- **Usage data**: timestamps of actions such as task creation, updates, and deletions

### 1.3 Information from third-party services

If you choose to log in via GitHub OAuth, we receive your GitHub username, email address, and profile picture from GitHub. We do not receive your GitHub password or access to your repositories.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide and operate the Application:

- To create and manage your account
- To display your profile and tasks to you and your team members
- To send in-app notifications about task activity
- To enforce role-based access control (admin / user)
- To respond to your requests or support inquiries

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your information may be shared in the following limited circumstances:

- **With team members**: your name, avatar, and task activity are visible to other authenticated users of the Application
- **As required by law**: if we are legally required to disclose information in response to a lawful request

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on a private server
- Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are hashed and salted using industry-standard algorithms
- All communication between your browser and the Application is encrypted via HTTPS
- API access requires authentication via session tokens or API keys

---

## 5. Cookies and Sessions

The Application uses session cookies to keep you logged in. These cookies are:

- **Strictly necessary**: required for authentication; the Application cannot function without them
- **Not used for tracking or advertising**

You can delete cookies at any time through your browser settings, which will log you out of the Application.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
- If you delete your account, your personal information will be removed from our systems within 30 days
- Task data created by you may remain visible to other team members after your account is deleted, attributed as "deleted user"

---

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction**: request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Data portability**: export your task data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
